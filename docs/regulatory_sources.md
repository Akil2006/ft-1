# Legal Metrology Official Sources

1. **India Code - Legal Metrology Act, 2009**
   - Source: Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India
   - Scope: Standard weights and measures, regulation of trade & commerce in packaged goods.

2. **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011**
   - Source: Gazette of India Notification GSR 202(E)
   - Scope: Mandatory declarations on pre-packaged commodities sold in India.

3. **Amendments (2017, 2021, 2022, 2023)**
   - Unit Sale Price rules, QR code declarations, E-Commerce compliance.
